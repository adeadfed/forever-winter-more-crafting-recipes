[size=6][b]More Crafting Recipes[/b][/size]

This mod gives little-used loot more purpose in The Forever Winter. It adds up to [b]150 crafting and disassembly recipes[/b] to the Innards workshop, so you can use what you already have and spend less time farming for a specific drop.

[size=5][b]Getting the recipes[/b][/size]

By default, you learn recipes from manuals. Bunco sells them under [b]Consumables[/b]. Each one unlocks a group of recipes, and you can buy them in any order.

If you turn on raid drops, you can also find manuals in crates. A manual you pick up unlocks its recipes when you extract to the Innards. If you die, you lose the manuals from that run.

You can also turn manuals off and have all the new recipes from the start. The usual facility and blueprint requirements still apply.

[size=5][b]Settings[/b][/size]

Close the game before editing [b]ue4ss\Mods\MoreCraftingRecipes\MoreCraftingRecipes.ini[/b].

[list]
[*][b]RecipeBooks[/b] — [b]Enabled = false[/b] unlocks all custom recipes. [b]RaidDrops = true[/b] adds manuals to crates. Raid drops are off by default.
[*][b]RecipeBookPrices[/b] — Price per manual, in whole credits without commas. Defaults range from 100,000 to 600,000 CR.
[*][b]RaidDropChances[/b] — Chance per eligible crate in each sector, from 0 to 100 percent.
[*][b]Crafting[/b] — [b]Instant = true[/b] by default for new stock and mod recipes. [b]false[/b] leaves craft times to the game or another timing mod. Tested with NoTimeGating.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]

Requires [b]The Forever Winter for Windows (64-bit)[/b] and [b]RE-UE4SS 4.0.0 Experimental[/b]. Tested with UE4SS commit [b]b2d936b9[/b]. [url=https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/releases]UE4SS downloads[/url] · [url=https://docs.ue4ss.com/dev/installation-guide.html]Installation guide[/url]

Close the game, install UE4SS, then extract the mod ZIP into [b]The Forever Winter\Windows\ForeverWinter\Binaries\Win64[/b]. Keep [b]MoreCraftingRecipes.ini[/b] and the [b]manuals.*[/b] files when replacing the mod folder.
